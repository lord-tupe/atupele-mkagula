About

Engineer. Creative. Visionary.

AM

Atupele Nathan Mkagula is a technology professional, systems architect, and creative technologist based in Malawi — building at the intersection of infrastructure, security, and innovation.

With deep expertise in cybersecurity, backend systems, and digital infrastructure, Atupele designs resilient architectures that serve both enterprise and community needs. His work bridges the gap between technical precision and creative vision — from securing critical systems to producing music that moves people.

Rooted in African ingenuity and driven by global ambition, he consults with organizations on digital transformation, builds scalable systems, and creates technology that tells a story.
